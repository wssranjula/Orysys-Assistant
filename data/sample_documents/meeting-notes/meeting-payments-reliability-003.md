# Payments Reliability Review - July 2026

## Purpose and scope

The review reconciled PAY-1224, PAY-1241, PAY-1260, and PAY-1288 against previously reported control completion.

## Operational detail

The board concluded that three controls had been overstated: the global connection budget excluded reconciliation, canary evidence excluded recovery consumers, and the failover dashboard measured authorization rather than full settlement recovery.

## Controls and response

OR-44 was closed only after a June gateway test, OR-51 remained open pending automated consumer coverage, and CG-9 was reassigned with an August deadline. Control owners must now attach runtime evidence before closure.
